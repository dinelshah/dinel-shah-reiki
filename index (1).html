About Dinel

Hello, my name is Dinel Shah and I am a holistic healer. I have been using holistic healing for myself and my family.

I am a certified holistic healer and Reiki practitioner, and I teach Reiki to teens, adults and older adults. My work starts with listening, to you and to what your body is asking for.

I encourage you to reach out about any personal, professional or financial situation you would like to see a shift in.

What is holistic healing?

Care for the whole person

Holistic healing is the practice of health and wellness that considers the entire person and all of the internal and external factors affecting them.

Be aware

Notice and follow what your body requires.

Honor small changes

Acknowledge even the tiniest change. Small shifts lead to greater ones.

Permission to receive

Healing often doesn't look like what you expect. Give yourself permission to receive it.
